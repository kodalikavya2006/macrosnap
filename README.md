# 🥗 MacroSnap - AI Food Nutrition Analyzer

MacroSnap is an AI-powered app that analyzes food photos to give instant calorie, protein, carbs, and fat breakdown.

**Problem it solves:** People struggle to track what they eat manually. MacroSnap makes it effortless - just snap a photo.

**How to use:** Upload a food image -> AI identifies food & gives macros -> Chat with AI for diet advice.

### ✨ Features
- 📸 Image-based food recognition using Gemini Vision
- 🔥 Instant calorie & macro estimation
- 💬 Chat with AI about your meal
- 📊 Clean Streamlit UI

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
- Python, Streamlit
- Google Gemini API
- Python-dotenv
